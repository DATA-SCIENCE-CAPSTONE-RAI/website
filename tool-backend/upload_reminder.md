# Upload reminder (#5)

Show this above the prompt-upload control, and keep the upload control disabled until the user
ticks the checkbox. Show it every time, not only on the first upload.

---

**Do not include personal or confidential data in your prompts.**

Prompts are sent to the model provider you choose, may be kept in their logs, and are stored
in this tool's results and exports. Before uploading, remove:

- names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth
- government ID numbers (SSN, driver's license, case or client numbers)
- health, financial, or benefits information about real people
- anything marked confidential or internal to your agency

☐ I confirm these prompts contain no personal or confidential data.

---

Accepted files: CSV or JSONL. Running uploaded prompts depends on the "Link prompts for testing"
feature in the evaluation backend, which is not built yet.
